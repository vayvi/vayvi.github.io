I am a 3rd year PhD student at [ENPC, IP Paris](https://imagine-lab.enpc.fr/), advised by [Mathieu Aubry](http://imagine.enpc.fr/~aubrym/). I am in ongoing collaboration with [Alexei Efros](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~efros/) (UC Berkeley).

I work on learning by decomposing images into simple, interpretable representations, and am seeking industry research roles in interpretable vision models.

Research
======
My recent work spans vectorization and object discovery, including bottom-up modeling of repeated elements via single-image analysis-by-synthesis, decomposing historical astronomical diagrams into geometric primitives, and detection-based text line recognition. See the [publications](/publications/) page for the full list.

Before my PhD, I completed the [M2 MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) (Mathématiques / Vision / Apprentissage) at ENS Paris-Saclay, as part of the Mathematics department (Normalienne élève).
